Good to know

  • The printed schedule says it is subject to change. Call 360-423-4770 or check longviewymca.org before you go.
  • Group exercise classes show their start time. The booklet does not print class lengths, except where a range is shown.
  • Classes with a cost need you to sign up first. Prices are shown as printed ($40/$65, $35/$70, $10/$40).
  • Main pool: there is always at least one lap lane open, sometimes two or three. That is why lap swim can overlap with water classes. Lap swim at 12 to 1pm on Mon, Wed and Fri was read from the chart without a printed time.
  • Swim lessons and swim team (4 to 6pm Mon to Thu) use 2 lap lanes from Nov 9 to Feb.
  • Groups are not allowed during open or family swims. The warm water pool is closed during family swims, special events, group programs and morning swim lessons, and stays are 30 minutes max.
  • Line Dancing with Ray on Mondays starts 9/14. Room codes: Back Gym, Main Gym, Cascade Room, Cycle Core Room.
